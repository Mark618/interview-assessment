Mina doesn’t send postcards anymore. She folds them.  
After a café fire and an unexpected guest, the only thing that keeps her grounded is a habit of leaving small paper cranes on trains.  
This is about kindness, not forgiveness. And the kind of love that sits quietly at your door until you’re ready.
